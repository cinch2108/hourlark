Privacy

Hourlark runs entirely in your browser. There are no accounts, and your timers, alarms, tasks, cities and settings are never uploaded to us or stored on any server.

What's stored on your device

To keep your tools working between visits, your browser's local storage keeps: your timers, stopwatch and laps, Pomodoro settings, tasks and daily session counts (last 30 days), countdown event, world clock cities, time zone converter cities, alarms, display preferences (12/24-hour, sounds, volume), the last tool you used and your cookie choice. You can delete all of it at any time by clearing site data for this site in your browser.

Notifications, sound and screen

Desktop notifications are off by default and are only requested after you click Enable notifications. Notifications are created by your own browser; we do not use push services and cannot send you anything when the page is closed. Sounds are generated on your device with the Web Audio API. Keep screen awake uses your browser's Wake Lock feature only while you have it ticked.

Share links

Countdown and time zone share links contain the event name, date, time and time zones in the web address itself. Anyone you send a link to can see those details, so don't put private information in an event name.

Advertising

Hourlark is free because it shows ads. Ad partners such as Google may use cookies to show and measure ads. If you choose "Essential only", only non-personalised ads are shown. Ads never have access to your timers, alarms, tasks or other data you enter. Learn how Google uses data at policies.google.com.

Hosting logs

Like any website, our hosting provider may keep standard request logs (such as IP address, browser type and the page requested) for security and reliability. Share-link details can appear in these logs because they are part of the address.

Children

Hourlark is a general audience tool and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
